Who We Are

The Only Partner
Who Runs It All —
And Does It Best.

SeaBeyond was founded in 2005 on a simple conviction: cruise line bingo had untapped potential as a high-margin, high-energy entertainment event — and the technology to realize that potential simply didn't exist.

We built it. Today, SeaBeyond is a fully integrated multimedia digital entertainment and revenue management platform — purpose-designed for the maritime environment, managed end-to-end by our team, supported around the clock.

We don't just supply technology. We become part of your operation — handling management, training, reporting, inventory, and guest experience so your team can stay focused on what they do best.

"Bingo is one of the highest-margin, lowest-overhead revenue events on any ship. Most programs barely scratch the surface."

SeaBeyond clients see measurable increases in bingo revenue — driven by higher participation, longer session engagement, and repeat attendance from guests who genuinely love the experience.

Pre-session screens advertise restaurants, excursions, and spa bookings to a captive, attentive audience. Every session is a revenue moment — even before the first ball drops.

The Platform

Four Integrated Systems.
One Seamless Experience.

Every component purpose-built for the cruise environment — not adapted from another industry. Runs on your ship, on your schedule, with our team behind it every step of the way.

01
🖥️

Custom POS System

Built specifically for cruise bingo — not adapted from retail or hospitality. Multiple synchronized terminals handle simultaneous multi-location sales, integrated with shipboard accounting and manifest systems.

  • Multi-terminal simultaneous sales across the venue
  • Loyalty programs, commission & budget tracking
  • WiFi integration with ship accounting & finance
  • Mobile cart with line-conditioning UPS for ship power
  • Custom cruise reporting & trending analysis
02
📡

Verifier Platform

A digital flashboard and certified random number generator that replaces the physical blower entirely. Winners are instantly verified — projected across all screens for complete, celebrated transparency.

  • Real-time digital flashboard on all venue screens
  • Certified random number generation
  • Instant, transparent winner verification
  • No disputes, no delays — pure excitement
03
📱

Digital Handheld Tablets

Rugged, purpose-built tablets guests love. Auto-play bingo synced live with the Verifier means every guest stays fully in the game and never misses a win.

  • Real-time auto-play synced live to the Verifier
  • Fireworks celebration screen triggers on every win
  • Patterns & called balls update automatically
  • Custom themes & branded ads per cruise
  • Mobile charging carts for multi-venue ships
04
🎬

Media & Entertainment System

A full broadcast command center turning every session into a genuine event — with cinematic media, winner celebrations, and cross-promotional messaging that drives revenue across the ship.

  • Animated ball-call videos & themed seasonal media
  • Pre-session display: pricing, cross-promos, dynamic messaging
  • Winner celebration videos & high-energy music
  • Trivia, sound effects, multi-language ball calling
  • Integrated raffle system with animated countdowns
